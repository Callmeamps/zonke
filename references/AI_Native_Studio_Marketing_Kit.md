# 1. Executive Summary

AI Native Studio is framed as a high-end, AI-forward operator brand with two specialist front ends: Real Estate and Tenders. The job is not to look like a software shop. The job is to look like the company that brings a business into the next era before competitors do.

The brand voice is sharp, premium, and slightly aggressive. The visual and verbal style should feel like a private-sector command centre: dark, clean, precise, and expensive.

-   Core message: AI-native or left behind.

-   Core promise: faster response, better control, stronger market position.

-   Core offer: consultation, integration, migration.

-   Delivery model: separate vertical brands, shared backend systems.

# 2. Market Context and Research Basis

Recent South African signals show why this positioning lands now: the country released a draft AI policy in April 2026, Microsoft announced a larger AI infrastructure push in South Africa, and a 2025 GenAI roadmap reported broad adoption momentum across organisations. That makes AI a live business conversation, not a future concept.

-   Use the message: managers do not want to be the ones who missed the shift.

-   Use the frame: modernity, speed, and relevance.

-   Avoid: bland efficiency language and generic consulting tone.

# 4. Sales Funnel

## Stage 1: Attention

-   Use guerrilla-style hooks that create surprise and contrast.

-   Channels: LinkedIn, WhatsApp status, direct mail, printed QR cards, local business areas, high-traffic office zones, property and procurement networks.

-   Message style: one line only, no paragraphs.

## Stage 2: Capture

-   Lead magnets: Real Estate Lead Leak Audit and Tender Miss Audit.

-   Destination: one simple landing page per vertical.

-   Conversion action: WhatsApp click, short form, or booking link.

## Stage 3: Qualify

-   10 to 15 minute call or voice-note exchange.

-   Sort leads into curious, problem-aware, ready, and serious.

## Stage 4: Demo

-   Show one visible AI moment: instant reply, lead routing, booking, deadline tracking, document sorting, or drafting assist.

-   Do not demo the whole stack first. Show one thing that feels like a leap.

## Stage 5: Close

-   Sell one of three entry points: Consultation, Integration, Migration.

-   Close on status and speed, not feature lists.

## Stage 6: Expand

-   After first deployment, extend to more staff, more workflows, and more departments.

-   Convert every launch into a case study and then into a clone.

# 5. Guerrilla Marketing Concepts

Guerrilla marketing works when it interrupts normal attention and forces a reaction. For this brand, that means making the market feel the pressure of being outdated.

-   One-line posters with a QR code and a hard headline.

-   Pocket cards handed out at business nodes, cafés, co-working spaces, and property events.

-   A public AI readiness scorecard for agencies and tender teams.

-   A short audit campaign: one pain question, one result, one call to action.

-   WhatsApp status reels with a futuristic, high-contrast look.

-   LinkedIn posts that challenge weak competitors directly.
