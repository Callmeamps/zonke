**AI Native Studio\
**Business plan, funnels, PRDs, offerings, pricing, and target list

**Verticals: Real Estate and Tenders**

## Working assumptions

-   Parent brand stays AI Native Studio.

-   Real Estate and Tenders are separate front-end businesses.

-   Shared backend handles systems, delivery, tooling, and reporting.

-   Selling point is AI-era relevance, speed, and visible advantage.

# 1. Executive summary

AI Native Studio is a parent brand that runs separate vertical businesses for Real Estate and Tenders. Each vertical gets its own message, funnel, and delivery stack, while the backend stays shared and efficient.

The sell is not generic automation. The sell is entering the AI era before competitors make the move look normal.

The market entry strategy uses guerrilla-style attention, fast qualification, simple demos, and a paid path into consultation, integration, and managed migration.

# 2. Brand architecture

## Parent brand

-   AI Native Studio = holding brand and central delivery engine.

-   Shared assets: automations, code, prompts, SOPs, dashboards, training material, and reporting.

-   Shared advantage: lower delivery cost and faster cloning across verticals.

## Front-end brands

-   Real Estate brand speaks to agencies, brokers, teams, and property offices.

-   Tender brand speaks to bid teams, construction firms, engineering firms, and service providers that live on documents and deadlines.

-   Each front-end should look like a specialist company, not a general agency.

# 4. Sales funnel

## 4.1 Funnel logic

-   Attention: guerrilla hooks, posters, flyers, WhatsApp clips, LinkedIn posts, and direct outreach.

-   Capture: one short landing page per vertical with a short form and WhatsApp click-to-chat.

-   Qualify: 10-15 minute call or voice-note exchange to sort curiosity from buying intent.

-   Demo: show one visible system doing one visible job.

-   Close: consultation, integration, or migration.

-   Expand: add more workflows, more departments, and more reporting.

## 4.2 Real Estate funnel

-   Hook: your competitor replies faster.

-   Lead magnet: 5-minute lead leak check.

-   Demo: instant lead response, qualification, viewing booking, follow-up sequence.

-   Close: pilot on one office or team.

-   Upsell: CRM clean-up, reporting, and team training.

## 4.3 Tenders funnel

-   Hook: missed tender = missed money.

-   Lead magnet: tender miss audit.

-   Demo: opportunity feed, deadline tracker, document sorting, AI-assisted draft support.

-   Close: pilot for one bid desk or admin team.

-   Upsell: broader workflow migration and supervision.

# 6. PRDs

## PRD 1: AI Lead Desk for Real Estate

-   Purpose: turn inbound property enquiries into fast, qualified conversations and booked viewings.

-   Users: agency owner, team lead, agent, admin assistant, buyer/seller.

-   Core features: instant response, lead routing, status tagging, reminder follow-up, booking links, basic dashboard.

-   Inputs: website forms, WhatsApp, email, call logs, manual lead entry.

-   Outputs: qualified lead list, booked appointments, follow-up history, response metrics.

-   MVP rule: one office, one workflow, one dashboard.

## PRD 2: Tender Signal Desk

-   Purpose: surface opportunities early and manage bid work without deadline drift.

-   Users: owner, bid manager, admin, technical lead.

-   Core features: opportunity feed, deadline tracker, document checklist, draft assistance, approval flow, submission log.

-   Inputs: public tender sources, internal files, email alerts, uploads.

-   Outputs: shortlist of opportunities, task list, submission status, deadline alerts.

-   MVP rule: one opportunity feed and one submission workflow.

## PRD 3: AI Native Command Center

-   Purpose: a shared backend view for both verticals.

-   Core features: user roles, metrics dashboard, prompt library, automation status, client health view.

-   Value: one operating layer reused across all future verticals.

# 10. Guerrilla marketing plays

-   One-line posters with a hard question: Are you AI-native yet?

-   Flyers with a QR code to the lead leak audit or tender miss audit.

-   WhatsApp status clips with a hard visual and one sentence.

-   LinkedIn posts calling out the pain directly, not soft thought leadership.

-   A free public scorecard for AI readiness or lead-response speed.

-   Short live teardown sessions of real estate lead flow or tender workflow.

# 11. Research basis used for the strategy

-   Guerrilla marketing works best when it is unexpected, low-cost, and memorable.

-   AI adoption pressure is increasing, and firms with visible AI strategies are seeing stronger revenue outcomes.
